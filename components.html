01 / Actions

Buttons

bc-button

Primary follows your accent (charcoal by default). Reserve danger for destructive actions and warning for actions needing caution.

<button class="bc-button bc-button--primary">Save changes</button>
<button class="bc-button bc-button--danger">Delete project</button>
<button class="bc-button bc-button--warning">Review changes</button>

06 / Focus

Dialog

bc-dialog

Use the native <dialog> element for focus management and Escape-to-close. Open it with dialog.showModal().

<dialog class="bc-dialog" id="example-dialog">
  <h2 class="bc-h2">New project</h2>
  <form method="dialog">
    <button class="bc-button">Close</button>
  </form>
</dialog>
<script>document.querySelector('#example-dialog').showModal()</script>
